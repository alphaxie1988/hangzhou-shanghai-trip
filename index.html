What the sense-check changed

Every stop was checked for opening hours, closing days, prices and travel time. Four things in the original plan would not have worked — all fixed below:

  • Hangzhou Paradise is closed all winter (30 Nov–5 Mar). Sat 19 Dec is now the China Animation Museum in the morning, then the Four Seasons indoor spa in the afternoon.
  • Shanghai Natural History Museum is closed on Tuesdays — it was planned for Tue 15 Dec. The Science and Technology Museum moves to Tue afternoon instead, and Wed 16 Dec becomes a full day at the Wild Animal Park. The Natural History Museum no longer fits this trip.
  • Sun 13 Dec tried to fit three venues that all close by 18:00 across town. Now: Xixi Wetland in the morning, Wetland Museum early afternoon, Airobox Tech 15:30–18:00. Airobox is not an evening visit — it closes at 18:00.
  • MeLand Club after dinner on Tue would leave ~1 hour for a full-price ticket. Replaced with a free Bund evening walk.

Smaller fixes: Mon 14 Dec train should be ~13:00 (hotel ~15:30, not 15:00); Yu Garden is closed Mondays, so the evening is bazaar + Wu Kang Lu only; Yongfu Temple needs a reservation made at least 1 day ahead; the Songcheng show is likely 19:30, back at the hotel ~21:30, and you need a luggage plan for that day.

A dark, elegant new-Chinese “garden” dining room — old stone slabs, pine and water features, semi-private booths. Ningbo's salty-fresh seafood meets Shaoxing's wine-soaked, fermented flavours. Named in the 2026 Michelin Guide, Hangzhou.

Must do: order the signature raw-marinated crab and let the staff shuck it at the table.
Must try: 十八斩生腌膏蟹 — Zhoushan red-paste crab cured 24 hours in Shaoxing yellow wine; sweet, silky, melts in the mouth.
Must eat: 臭豆腐烧白蟹 (stinky tofu braised with white crab), 蒋氏干菜焖肉 (braised pork belly with dried mustard greens, in a bun), and fresh-baked 榨菜月饼.

A Shanghai crab institution since the 1950s, with its own Yangcheng Lake crab farms. Ming–Qing dining room, red lanterns, old wood, live folk music in the evenings. Free expert crab-shelling at the table.

Call ahead: mid-December is the tail end of hairy-crab season — supply drops and quality gets inconsistent. Confirm crab is still available for 14 Dec, and order male crabs (peak mid-Nov–early Dec).

Must do: let the staff shell the crab for you, and order warm yellow wine with ginger — the traditional pairing.
Must try: 清蒸大闸蟹 — steamed hairy crab. December is past peak season, but they serve crab all year.
Must eat: the “four greats” — steamed crab claw, crab-leg meat with asparagus, stir-fried crab roe, crab paste with rice-noodle skins — plus 秃黄油捞饭 (rice in rich crab-roe oil; add the vinegar).

Sweet, delicate Jiangnan cooking with beautiful plating and staff in hanfu. The seafood rice is finished with hot broth poured at the table. The mall is a parent-child mall, so it's good with kids.

Branch correction: the Changning branch is at Hongqiao Nanfeng City (150 Zunyi Rd), not near Zhongshan Park / Jiangsu Road as first listed. It's on Metro Line 2 — the same line as the Science and Technology Museum that afternoon.

Must do: order a set menu for 2–4 people — it covers the signatures at about 25% off.

Songcheng — Thu 17 Dec

Half an afternoon: straight from Hangzhou East station (~30 min by taxi), arrive ~3:30pm. Explore the Song-dynasty streets, then the Romance of the Song Dynasty show (about 1 hour). The evening show is usually 19:30 — confirm the exact time when booking, as sources disagree (19:00 / 19:30). Back at the hotel ~21:00–21:30.

Luggage: you check out of Shanghai with bags and only check into the Holiday Inn after the show. Either use left-luggage at Hangzhou East, or drop bags at the hotel first (adds ~1 h round trip; still leaves you at Songcheng ~16:00, fine for a 19:30 show).

Family spa — Sat 19 Dec

The Spa at Four Seasons Hotel Hangzhou at West Lake, 5 Lingyin Road — fully indoors: heated indoor pool (10 × 19 m), nine treatment rooms, sauna and steam, plus a free kids' club (ages 2–12, 09:00–11:30 and 13:00–17:00). Spa daily 10:00–22:00. Treatments must be booked at least 24 hours ahead — call +86 571 8829 8888. Treatments from ~¥580 (30-min bath) / ¥680 (40-min scrub); massages more. Indoor-pool access usually comes with a treatment booking — confirm when you call. Taxi from the hotel ~15–20 min.

Book before you go

Most of these use real-name timed booking, released 7 days ahead. Trip.com sells foreigner-friendly tickets for several of them if a WeChat mini-program won't accept a passport.

  • Shanghai Science and Technology Museum — tickets released 7 days ahead at 10:00, real-name booking only, no on-site ticket office. Book on Tue 8 Dec. Tickets
  • Yongfu Temple (Fri 18 Dec) — free, but a real-name reservation is mandatory, 1–7 days ahead, via the “Hangzhou Lingyin Feilai Peak” mini-program. No same-day booking. Book by Thu 17 Dec at the latest.
  • Songcheng show (Thu 17 Dec) — book the dated combo ticket (park + show) in advance, ~¥320 standard / ¥350 VIP. Confirm whether the 17 Dec show is 19:00 or 19:30. Don't buy the cheaper night-tour-only ticket by mistake.
  • Xixi Wetland Park — real-name booking, opens 7 days ahead; ¥80 adult (¥70 if booked a day ahead on official WeChat), electric boat ¥60 extra.
  • The Spa at Four Seasons (Sat 19 Dec) — treatments must be booked at least 24 hours ahead: call +86 571 8829 8888. From ~¥580 per treatment; confirm indoor-pool access for non-guests when you book.
  • 成隆行·蟹王府 dinner (Mon 14 Dec) — call 021-63212010 to book and confirm hairy crab is still available in mid-December.
  • High-speed trains (14 & 17 Dec) — book on 12306 or Trip.com; Hangzhou East ↔ Shanghai Hongqiao, ~¥73 second class.
  • Impression West Lake show (Sat 12 Dec, optional) — only if you want the evening show (~19:15 in Dec, ¥360 standard). It would replace the night market or dinner, not sit alongside both. Season ends mid-December.

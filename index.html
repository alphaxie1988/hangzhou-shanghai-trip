榆园·甬绍荟馆 (杭州嘉里中心店)

Slotted: Sat 12 Dec, dinner — after Wulin Night Market, same Wulin area. Kerry Centre, Yan'an Road, North Zone Commercial 1F. Metro Line 1, Fengqi Road Station, Exit C1. Hours 11:00–22:30. Mon–Thu 14:00–17:00 afternoon tea only. 4.6★ · ¥169/person · Zhejiang cuisine (甬绍菜 — Ningbo + Shaoxing).

A dark, elegant new-Chinese “garden” dining room — old stone slabs, pine and water features, semi-private booths. Ningbo's salty-fresh seafood meets Shaoxing's wine-soaked, fermented flavours. Named in the 2026 Michelin Guide, Hangzhou.

Must do: order the signature raw-marinated crab and let the staff shuck it at the table.
Must try: 十八斩生腌膏蟹 — Zhoushan red-paste crab cured 24 hours in Shaoxing yellow wine; sweet, silky, melts in the mouth.
Must eat: 臭豆腐烧白蟹 (stinky tofu braised with white crab), 蒋氏干菜焖肉 (braised pork belly with dried mustard greens, in a bun), and fresh-baked 榨菜月饼.
Recommended by Cylin

成隆行·蟹王府 (九江路店)

Slotted: Mon 14 Dec, dinner — your first night in Shanghai. 216 Jiujiang Road, Huangpu, ~250m from Nanjing East Road Metro. Hours 11:00–15:00, 17:00–22:00. Tel 021-63212010. ¥311/person on Dianping; budget ¥400–600 for a full crab feast. Michelin 1 star.

A Shanghai crab institution since the 1950s, with its own 50,000-mu crab farm. Ming–Qing dining room, red lanterns, old wood, live folk music in the evenings. Free expert crab-shelling at the table.

Must do: let the staff shell the crab for you, and order warm yellow wine with ginger — the traditional pairing.
Must try: 清蒸大闸蟹 — steamed hairy crab. December is past peak season (females Sep–Oct, males Oct–Nov), but they serve crab all year.
Must eat: the “four greats” — steamed crab claw, crab-leg meat with asparagus, stir-fried crab roe, crab paste with rice-noodle skins — plus 秃黄油捞饭 (rice in rich crab-roe oil; add the vinegar).
Recommended by Cylin

米桃雅宴·江南苏锡菜

Slotted: Tue 15 Dec, dinner — 长宁店 (Changning), near Zhongshan Park / Jiangsu Road. Suzhou/Wuxi (Jiangnan) cuisine. Branches: 长宁店 4.7★ ¥163 · 万象城店 4.8★ ¥163 · 久光中心店 4.4★ ¥132 · 静安合生MOHO店 4.6★ ¥128. Sets: ¥329 for 2, ¥559 for 3–4.

Sweet, delicate Jiangnan cooking with beautiful plating and staff in hanfu. The seafood rice is finished with hot broth poured at the table.

Must do: order a set menu for 2–4 people — it covers the signatures at about 25% off.
Must try: 松鼠桂鱼 (squirrel fish) — mandarin fish scored so it fans out like a squirrel's tail, crisp outside, snowy inside, sweet-and-sour glaze (about ¥188).
Must eat: 招牌无锡蟹粉小笼包 (crab-roe soup dumplings), 活杀响油鳝糊 (sizzling eel paste), 海鲜乌米泡饭 (seafood black-rice soup rice), and 阳山桃胶酒酿圆子 for dessert.
